How we work

Our service process

The same four steps whether it is a single bathroom or a 40-unit exterior.

  1. 01

    Consultation & site visit

    A no-obligation walkthrough. We look at the actual conditions, talk through what you want, and tell you what is realistic.

  2. 02

    Scope & written estimate

    You get a clear scope and a written estimate — what is included, what it costs, and how long it takes. No moving targets.

  3. 03

    Build & daily communication

    Our crews work to the schedule. You know who is on site, what happens next, and you can reach us the whole way through.

  4. 04

    Walkthrough & clean-up

    We walk the finished work with you, handle the punch list, and leave the space clean — a detail our reviews keep mentioning.
