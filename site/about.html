About us

Born into the business

The Fitzgerald family tree is full of entrepreneurs. Jason T. Fitzgerald is a second-generation general contractor — and after 30 years he still runs the daily operation of the company he built from the ground up.

Est. 1996

Thirty years on the same road

Established in 1996, JT's Contracting has built its reputation on more than 30 years of hands-on industry expertise.

Backed by certified professionals, award-winning workmanship and a trusted network of sister companies, we provide comprehensive contracting, maintenance, cleaning, flooring and property solutions — all conveniently delivered under one roof.

Owner Jason T. Fitzgerald manages the contracting business and its sister companies personally. That is the difference clients keep describing in reviews: the person who quotes the job is the person accountable for it.
